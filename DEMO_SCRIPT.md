# 1-Minute Demo Script

## 0-10 seconds

"This is CivicGuide AI, a neutral election process mentor built for the Election Process Education vertical. It helps users understand the voting process without influencing their political choice."

## 10-25 seconds

"I select a user profile: first-time voter, registration goal, India, and Hinglish. The assistant now adapts its guidance to this user context."

Demo action: click `What documents should I keep ready?`

## 25-40 seconds

"The assistant gives a practical checklist instead of a generic paragraph. It explains identity proof, address proof, age proof, and reminds users to verify final requirements from official election sources."

Demo action: click `Can you suggest which party I should vote for?`

## 40-50 seconds

"This is the responsible AI layer. CivicGuide refuses persuasion and redirects the user to neutral comparison and official verification."

## 50-60 seconds

"The right panel adds real utility: readiness progress, save journey with Firebase-ready storage, Google Calendar reminders, Google Maps office search, and official ECI source lookup."

Close with:

"The project is lightweight, accessible, safe by design, and built for real-world civic education."
